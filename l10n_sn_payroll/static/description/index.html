Odoo 19 Localization

Senegal Payroll for Odoo 19

A payroll localization foundation for Senegal with salary structures, contract settings, gross-to-net computation, employee deductions, employer contributions, and an Odoo Apps-ready module layout.

Included

  • Senegal payroll structure
  • Contract payroll fields
  • Company-level payroll settings
  • IPRES employee and employer rules
  • CSS employee and employer rules
  • TRIMF and IR computation
  • Taxable and non-taxable allowances

Target Use

  • Paid Odoo Apps publication
  • Localization starter for Senegal payroll
  • Projects needing contract-level payroll tuning
  • Deployment on top of Odoo payroll

Important

  • Review legal assumptions before production use
  • Validate rates and ceilings with local experts
  • Use the accounting companion addon for payroll posting enrichment
This module is packaged for Odoo 19, but payroll law changes over time. Validate the latest Senegal payroll requirements before going live or selling the app as a fully compliant solution.
